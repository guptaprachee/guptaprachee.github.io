01
AWS · VIDEO PROCESSING

Making video color-space decisions defensible.

Owned a two-phase derivation engine from design through testing and delivery. Phase 1 used standards-based lookups; Phase 2 offered customer opt-in heuristic derivation.

End-to-end ownership · Phase 1 shipped to production

The decision behind the design

For Phase 2, I advocated an elimination approach instead of a weighted scoring system. We did not have enough evidence to justify the weighting thresholds. I worked with the team to reach agreement on an approach that avoided unsupported scoring assumptions.

My ownership included design documents, implementation, unit and integration testing, cross-functional coordination, production delivery of Phase 1, and a future-work document.
